# Clinical Operational Guidelines for Appointment Management

## Section 1: Standard Protocols (Low Risk)
For patients identified as Low Risk for no-shows:
- Standard scheduling is sufficient.
- Send a standard automated SMS reminder 24 hours before the appointment.
- No immediate human intervention is necessary.

## Section 2: Targeted Protocols (Medium Risk)
For patients identified as Medium Risk for no-shows:
- Send targeted SMS reminders 48 hours and 24 hours before the appointment.
- Provide a clear, easy-to-use link in the SMS allowing the patient to confirm or reschedule their appointment without calling the office.
- Monitor response; if no confirmation is received by 24 hours prior, flag for front-desk awareness.

## Section 3: High Intervention Protocols (High Risk)
For patients identified as High Risk for no-shows:
- Patients with high no-show risk should receive a personal phone call from the front desk staff 48 hours prior.
- Offer flexible rescheduling options during the call if they express barriers to attendance.
- If transportation is a known issue for the demographic/neighborhood, offer information on medical transport or telehealth alternatives.
- The clinic is authorized to selectively double-book or overbook appointment slots adjacent to High Risk patients to maximize provider utilization.
